3D reconstruction for image segmentation by single view, vanishing point, and line of view.
Thanks to the team members 
Luca Martin, Hassan R. Khan, Fatma Tej, Yi Yue, Philipp Wang, Ali Rabeh

Required Toolbox: 
Image Processing Toolbox

1: Run main.exe
2: Select image or upload your own image
3: Press the select foreground object button by marking points around the 
   region of interest. If the foreground object is fully selected, 
   DOUBLE-CLICK on the last selected point. You will see the mask and the 
   ROIon the right side.
4: If you finished the selection you have the option to select another 
   foreground object. If you are finished with the selection, click on the 
   arrow. 
5: In step 2 start by selecting the vanishing point and backtround in the 
   picture. 
6: Press on "click here to compute the mesh" to visualize the resulting 
   mesh on the image. 
7: You can still adjust the mesh by pulling the corners of the rectangle if
   you are not satisfied with the result. 
8: After defining a proper mesh, click on the 360° button to create the 
   3D view of the image
9: After a short calculation time, the 3D-scene appears. 
10: Feel free to move around with the mouse or with the sliders in the GUI
11: The eye button resets the azimuth and elevation of the plot
